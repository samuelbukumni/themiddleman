# Code of Conduct

The Middleman's team is small, mostly volunteer-driven, and built on trust —
the same value we're building into the marketplace itself. This document
sets basic expectations for anyone contributing, on GitHub, Discord, or
elsewhere in team spaces.

## Our Standard

- Be respectful. Disagree on ideas, not people.
- Give and receive feedback constructively — especially code review feedback.
- Assume good faith. Most misunderstandings are just that.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks, in any team space.
- Respect confidentiality — internal docs, unreleased features, and business
  details discussed in the team Discord stay within the team unless Samuel
  says otherwise.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory jokes or language, or personal attacks
- Publicly disclosing confidential project information without authorization
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or team coordination
- Any conduct that would reasonably make another contributor feel unsafe or unwelcome

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct,
report it to Samuel directly at hello@themiddleman.com.ng. Reports will be
handled with discretion.

## Enforcement

Team leads may take any action deemed appropriate, including a warning,
temporary suspension from team spaces, or removal from the project, depending
on the severity and pattern of the behavior.

We're building something meant to earn trust at scale. That starts with how
we treat each other while building it.
